Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Oriel. On Android: Google Play → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → Oriel. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

I changed phones. How do I get Pro back?
Sign in with the same Apple or Google account, then tap Restore purchases on the Pro screen.

How do I delete my account?
Profile → Manage (next to your email) → Delete account. Your chart positions, connections and Oriel AI’s notes are removed from our servers. Your journal stays on your phone until you delete the app.

Is my journal private?
Yes. It stays on your phone. Oriel AI reads it only if you turn on “Let Oriel read my journal”, and you can turn that off at any time.

I don’t know my birth time.
Oriel still works. Your Rising sign needs it, and Oriel says which placements are uncertain without it.
