Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 November 2026

Oriel is an astrology and journal app for iPhone and Android. This policy explains what Oriel keeps, where, and why. The short version: your birth details, your journal and your voice notes stay on your phone. A few things go to our servers so your account, your friends and Oriel AI can work. We don’t sell your data, show ads, or use your data to train AI.

Questions: theorielcosmos@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

  • Your birth date, time and place. Oriel works out your chart on the phone.
  • Your journal: entries, the questions you answered, moods, dreams, voice notes and their transcripts. Voice notes are turned into text on the phone; your voice is never sent anywhere.
  • People you add by hand, with their birth details.
  • Your settings and your Oriel AI chat history.

These are removed when you delete the app. You can copy them out at any time with Profile → Export my data.

What your account keeps

Everyone signs in to Oriel with Apple or Google. Your account is held by Supabase, our database provider, on servers in the United States. It keeps:

  • Your email address. With Apple’s “Hide My Email”, that’s a private relay address. If you sign in with Google, Google also sends your name and photo; we don’t keep them.
  • The first name and username you choose in Oriel.
  • Your chart positions (the sign and degree of each planet), not your birth date, time or place. Connected friends see these.
  • Your connections, invites and friend requests.
  • Oriel AI’s short notes about you (see below), if you keep that on.

Oriel AI

Oriel AI is off until you turn it on and agree to what it sends. When you ask a question or ask for a dream reflection, Oriel’s server (on Google Cloud in the United States) sends this to Google’s Gemini models on Vertex AI to write the reply:

  • your chart positions, today’s sky and the app’s outlook lines for the weeks ahead;
  • your question, the recent messages in that chat and a short summary of earlier ones;
  • the card or reading you asked from;
  • for people you mention: their first name, who they are to you, their Sun, Moon and Rising signs and the app’s reading for you two, never their birth details;
  • a dream, when you ask about that dream;
  • only if you turn on Let Oriel read my journal (Oriel Pro): up to five journal entries and three dreams from the past week, shortened.

Our server doesn’t keep any of this after replying. It keeps only counts of how many questions you’ve asked, so it can apply your plan’s limits. Google processes the request under its Cloud terms and doesn’t use it to train its models.

Notes Oriel keeps. So Oriel remembers you between chats, it keeps up to twelve short notes (for example “Has an interview on Friday 27 November”). They’re kept on your phone and your account, and you can read, delete or switch them off in Profile → What Oriel remembers. They never include health, money, religion, addresses or anything you ask Oriel to forget.

Purchases

Oriel Pro and Pro+ are bought through the App Store or Google Play, which handle payment. RevenueCat, our purchases provider, receives your purchase records linked to your Oriel account so your plan follows you between phones. We never see your card details.

Notifications

The morning note is scheduled on your phone. No notification service receives your data.

Setup counts

Oriel counts, without any account, device or personal detail, how many people reach each step of setting up the app. Your phone sends only the name of the step, such as “birth details saved”, and which astrology you picked, once each. Our server adds it to that day’s total and keeps nothing else, so we can see where setup is confusing and fix it.

What we don’t do

  • No ads and no advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No analytics that follow you, and no selling or sharing of your data with data brokers.
  • No training of AI models on your data.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Question counts are kept while your account exists. Setup counts are daily totals with nothing about you in them, kept for up to 400 days. Server logs, which never contain your messages, are kept for up to 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Profile → Manage (next to your email) → Delete account. This removes your account, chart positions, connections and Oriel AI’s notes from our servers straight away. If you signed in with Apple, Oriel is also removed from your Apple Account.
  • Export: Profile → Export my data.
  • Oriel AI: turn it off in Profile at any time; nothing is sent while it’s off.
  • To ask what we hold about you, or to correct or delete it, email theorielcosmos@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may have further rights, including complaining to your data protection authority.

Children

Oriel isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has an account, email us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above, and tell you in the app when the change matters.
